## Assessment structure

- **Unit Quizzes — 55% of final grade.** Seven 25-question quizzes, Scantron-scored, ~35 students, given approximately every two chapters.
- **Cumulative Final Exam — 20%.** Covers the whole course; "may include multiple-choice questions and other formats appropriate to the material" — unlike the unit quizzes, the final is not necessarily MC-only.
- **Attendance — 20%.**
- **Additional Assignments — 5%.**

## Unit quiz → chapter mapping

Derived from the Tentative Course Schedule. Each quiz falls at the end of its second chapter's week, so it covers that chapter plus the one before it:

| Quiz | Chapters covered | Chapter titles |
|---|---|---|
| Unit Quiz 1 | Ch. 1 + Ch. 2 | History & Approaches; Research Methods & Statistics |
| Unit Quiz 2 | Ch. 3 + Ch. 4 | Neuroscience & Biological Bases; Sensation & Perception |
| Unit Quiz 3 | Ch. 5 + Ch. 6 | Consciousness; Sleep |
| Unit Quiz 4 | Ch. 7 + Ch. 8 | Learning; Memory |
| Unit Quiz 5 | Ch. 9 + Ch. 10 | Thinking, Language & Intelligence; Lifespan Development |
| Unit Quiz 6 | Ch. 11 + Ch. 12 | Social Psychology; Emotion, Stress & Coping |
| Unit Quiz 7 | Ch. 13 + integrative material | Psychological Disorders & Therapy, "along with selected integrative material from across the course" |
| Final Exam | Cumulative | All chapters; may include non-MC formats |

**This means every per-chapter exam-bank blueprint (`pipeline/exam-bank-spec.md`) needs to size its target "keep" count against half of a 25-question quiz, shared with its paired chapter, not a full 25-question quiz to itself** — except Ch. 13, which shares Quiz 7 with unspecified "integrative material" rather than a second numbered chapter, and the Final, which draws from all thirteen.
