# Chapter 6 — slide-only image manifest

Images used in the Chapter 6 lecture deck but **not** in the book. Book figures keep
their own records in `docs/images/ch06/`; nothing here is part of the textbook's
figure set.

Every image needs a row before the deck can be treated as distributable. An image
with `provenance: UNKNOWN` is fine for your own classroom and is not safe to hand to
another instructor — a deck shared with an adopter is redistribution, and the book's
figures carry attribution records precisely because that matters.

## Conventions

- Cap the long edge at 2560px. That covers a 4K projector; the book's own figures are
  already larger than any display needs.
- Illustrations and photographs go in as JPEG, flat diagrams as PNG. The Chapter 6
  book figures are 1536×1024 PNGs at ~1.8 MB each and compress to roughly 280 KB as
  JPEG with no visible difference on a projector.
- The deck style guide's "no decorative stock images" rule
  (`docs/images/psych101_figure_style_guide.md`) governs the *book*. Slides can be
  looser — but a decorative image still needs a provenance row.
