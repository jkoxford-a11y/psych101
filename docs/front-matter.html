AI Collaboration Statement

This textbook was developed through extensive collaboration with generative and agentic AI systems. AI tools contributed substantially to drafting, revision, research support, source checking, coding, visual development, instructional design, and quality assurance. I directed the work, made the substantive and editorial decisions, and take responsibility for the final content.

Production Notes

The tools below were used across different phases of this project; each entry names the tool and the kind of work it did. All contributions were directed, reviewed, and edited by the author, as described in the Collaboration Statement above.

  • Claude (Cowork) — initial data ingestion and course-material planning.
  • Claude Code — website development and chapter drafting. Took over the drafting role originally planned for Cowork after a sandbox limitation made in-repo editing impractical there.
  • Claude (subagent audits) — independent, no-memory review passes, including blind item audits for the exam-bank pipeline.
  • ChatGPT — independent auditing and image generation.
  • Codex — independent auditing and image generation.
  • Gemini — external, book-level audits of conceptual coherence across chapters, and independent blind item audits for the exam-bank pipeline.

This list reflects tools used as of the Fall 2026 edition and will be updated as tooling changes in future editions.
